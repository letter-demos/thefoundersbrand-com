Product

The Founders Brand is a boutique branding studio built specifically for early-stage startup founders. We help entrepreneurs craft sharp identities, clear messaging, and visual strategies that command attention from investors, early adopters, and press from the very beginning.

Founder Identity Sprint

A focused two-week engagement that gives early-stage founders a complete brand foundation: logo, color palette, typography, and a one-paragraph positioning statement ready for pitch decks and landing pages.

  • Discovery session to define your audience, competitors, and tone
  • Three logo concepts with one round of revisions
  • Full color palette and type system delivered as a brand style guide
  • One-paragraph positioning statement and tagline
  • Delivery of all files in print and web formats
  • Completed in 14 business days or less

Investor Narrative Package

We craft the words that make your pitch deck land. This package develops your founding story, core value proposition, and investor-facing messaging so you walk into every meeting with a clear and compelling narrative.

  • One-hour founder interview to extract your story and vision
  • Refined founding story suitable for pitch decks and About pages
  • Core value proposition and three supporting proof points
  • Investor-ready one-liner and elevator pitch script
  • Homepage headline and subheadline copy
  • One revision round included

Launch Ready Brand System

Our most comprehensive offering, designed for founders who want a complete, professional brand presence before they go public with their startup. Everything from identity to website copy to social media guidelines.

  • Everything in Founder Identity Sprint and Investor Narrative Package
  • Full brand guidelines document covering voice, tone, and visual usage
  • Website copywriting for up to five pages
  • LinkedIn and Twitter profile copy optimized for founder visibility
  • Social media visual templates in Figma or Canva
  • 30-minute brand walkthrough call upon delivery
  • Delivered within 30 business days

Brand Audit and Refresh

Already launched but feeling like your brand is holding you back? We assess what you have, identify where it falls short, and deliver a prioritized roadmap for rebuilding credibility and consistency.

  • Review of existing logo, website, pitch deck, and social profiles
  • Written audit report with specific findings and gap analysis
  • Competitive positioning review against three to five direct competitors
  • Prioritized action plan with quick wins and long-term improvements
  • One follow-up strategy call to walk through recommendations
