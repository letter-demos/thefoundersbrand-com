About The Founders Brand

The Founders Brand was started in 2019 by Cassidy Merrow, a brand strategist who spent eight years at a mid-size agency in Chicago watching early-stage startups get squeezed out of branding conversations because they could not afford enterprise retainers. Cassidy had worked with consumer brands and established tech companies, but the clients she found most energizing were always the scrappy founders trying to build something from scratch. She left the agency in January of that year with one freelance client, a rented desk in a co-working space, and a clear conviction: there was no good option for founders who needed serious brand work at an early-stage price.

What started as a one-person consultancy quickly attracted a small team. Copywriter and messaging strategist Leo Farrant joined in 2020, followed by visual designer Amara Ng in 2021. Today the studio works with a rotating group of four to six contractors and maintains a consistent team of three full-time staff. Every client engagement is led by Cassidy or Leo directly. The team has worked with founders across fintech, healthtech, logistics, edtech, and direct-to-consumer products, completing over 180 brand engagements since launch.

The company is based in Austin, Texas, though roughly two thirds of its clients are remote. The Founders Brand does not take equity, does not upsell retainers to clients who do not need them, and does not work with more than twelve active clients at any given time. That constraint is intentional. The team believes that good branding work for early-stage companies requires close attention, honest feedback, and a genuine stake in the founder's success — none of which scale well past a certain volume.

What we value

  • Founders first: every decision we make is filtered through what actually serves the founder, not what makes our work look impressive in a portfolio
  • Clarity over cleverness: we prefer plain, direct language and straightforward visual choices over trends that age poorly
  • Honest counsel: we will tell you when your positioning is off, your name is confusing, or your instinct is leading you somewhere we do not think will work
  • Right-sized work: we scope every engagement to what a founder genuinely needs at their current stage, not what we could sell them
  • Speed without sloppiness: early-stage founders move fast and we keep up, but we never ship work we are not proud to put our name on
